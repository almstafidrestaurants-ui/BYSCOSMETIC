Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information is handled when you use the BYS Cosmetics store through our iOS app or website.

Information you provide

You can browse the catalog without creating an account. If you register, we use your email address to verify and manage your account. Depending on the profile type you select, we may collect your name, phone number, country, region, address, and professional or business details you enter. If you choose to use the location feature and grant permission, your current location can be added to your profile as a map link.

How we use your information

We use this information to verify sign-ins, maintain your profile, handle orders and payment confirmations, and respond to requests. The store team may receive registration notices containing your name, account type, and address or region. Your browser or the app’s web view can store preferences, favorites, and cart information on your device.

Service providers and payments

We use Google Firebase for account authentication and profile storage and a verification service to deliver and check sign-in or registration codes. If you choose to pay by manual transfer through Whish, the transfer takes place through Whish, whose own privacy terms apply. We may use information you provide about the transfer to confirm payment.

Retention and your choices

We keep account information as needed to maintain your account and provide the service. We may retain records needed to complete transactions, resolve disputes, or meet applicable obligations. You can decline location access in your device settings and contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated personal information, or to withdraw consent where applicable.
